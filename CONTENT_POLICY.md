# Content policy

Scribble is meant to be fun and usable as a profile picture anywhere. These rules decide what goes into the catalog of pieces and words.

## Always original

- Every piece is drawn from scratch for this project.
- No tracing or copying of existing artwork, memes, mascots, logos or characters from films, games, comics or anime. A *style* can be inspired by something; a *character* cannot be reproduced.

## Brands

Real brand names are recognised by the parser only so that they can be **replaced** with an invented logo (for example a hoodie mentioning a sportswear brand gets a made-up name printed on it). Real logos, trademarks or brand names are never drawn.

## Not in the catalog

- Hate symbols, slurs, or imagery associated with hate groups.
- Pieces that caricature an ethnicity, religion, nationality, gender or disability.
- Sexual content.
- Real people's faces.

## Handled with care

- **Religious and cultural items** (hijab, turban…) are drawn respectfully and are never tied to a gender or a skin tone automatically.
- **Gender** is a set of independent traits (lashes, lips, hair…), not a switch. Words like "girl" or "woman" only set starting values that the user can change.
- **Tobacco and similar items** exist because people ask for them in avatars; they are never added unless the user writes them.

## Reporting

If you find a piece or a word that breaks these rules, open an issue or contact the maintainers privately.
