# Brand

- `mark.svg`: the Describble mark, a face whose nose is a text cursor (writing turns into a drawing). Ink `#1b1b1f` on paper `#f6efe1`, drawn by hand for this project. It is the favicon and sits next to the wordmark in the site header.
- `apple-touch-icon.png` (180×180) and `og.png` (1200×630, the preview shown when the site is shared) are rendered from the mark, the wordmark in Bricolage Grotesque and three avatars drawn by the engine. `build.mjs` copies them into `dist/site/`.
- `docs/social-preview.png` (1280×640) is the GitHub social preview, uploaded by hand in the repository settings.

The name and these marks are reserved to the project: they are not covered by the MIT or CC0 licenses (see `LICENSE-ASSETS`). Forks should use their own name and logo.
