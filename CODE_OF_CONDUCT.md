# Code of Conduct

We want Scribble to be a friendly place for everyone, whatever their experience, background or identity.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and assume good intent.
- Give and receive feedback on the work, not the person.
- Welcome newcomers and help them get their first contribution in.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory jokes and imagery.
- Personal attacks, trolling, or deliberately disruptive behaviour.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.
- Contributing artwork or words meant to mock or demean a group of people.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, code or artwork that break these rules and may block people who repeatedly do so. To report a problem, contact the maintainers privately through the contact listed on the repository profile.

This code of conduct is inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
